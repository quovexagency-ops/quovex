The part nobody sees
Editing eats your week.

One good minute of video can take an afternoon to cut: finding the best take, trimming every pause, captions, music, the hook in the first second, then exporting it again for every app. So most people either post less than they planned, or post things they’re not proud of.

Losing your weekend to editing.
If you make content

You get your hours back for the part only you can do.

The ideas, the filming, being in front of the camera. You post more often, and you put your energy into what people actually follow you for.

If you run a business

You get your hours back for the business.

Sales calls, customers, the product. Your videos keep going out every week without you sitting up at midnight dragging clips around.
