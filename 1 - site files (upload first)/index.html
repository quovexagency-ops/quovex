Why we do this

Why do good products get ignored?

Most founders we talk to have a good product. Explaining it is the hard part. A stranger gives your video about eight seconds, or your homepage one scroll, and that’s where a lot of products lose them: too many features, too much jargon, or a script written by someone who knows the product too well to explain it simply. That’s the part we fix.

We make onboarding and launch videos, short clips for social, and longer YouTube videos. Your project doesn’t get passed between five people. Whoever you talk to on day one is the one editing your video.

People don’t buy what they don’t understand.

A big production budget doesn’t help if nobody watches to the end. What counts is whether someone finishes your video, or reads your homepage instead of leaving. That’s what we work on. See our work →
