Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains what information QUOVEX collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. In short: we only collect what you send us, we use it to reply to you and do the work, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote: your name, email, phone number (optional), website and reference link (optional), what you need, when you need it, the package you picked, and your project details.
  • When you leave a review: your name, your star rating and, if you write one, your review.

We don’t use advertising trackers or analytics on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a price.
  • To plan and deliver your project if you hire us.
  • With your permission, to show your review on our website.

Where it’s kept

Quote requests and reviews are saved in our project database on Supabase and emailed to our own inbox (Gmail) through EmailJS. If that ever fails, a backup copy is emailed through Web3Forms. These services handle the data on our behalf and don’t use it for anything else.

Videos on this site

Some videos are played through YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) and Vimeo. When you play one of those, that service may set its own cookies. Videos hosted on our own site don’t.

The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them, like any website you visit.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to talk to you about your project, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you’re in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, that’s the ICO).

Contact

For anything about your data, send us a message through the quote form and write “Privacy” in the project details. We’ll reply within one business day.
